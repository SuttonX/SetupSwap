Initial public release of SetupSwap for World of Warcraft: Wrath of the Lich King 3.3.5a.

Save and restore account-wide setups with addon selections, captured addon settings and positions, chat layouts, and native WoW keybindings.  Use profiles for desktop/controller play, questing, raiding, PvP, other activities, or a restore point before experimenting with your current setup.

Switch through the settings window, slash commands, or minimap button.  Includes an early-startup helper, a second-reload fallback, explicit overwrite choices, and a copyable `/ss log` report.

Fully close WoW and extract both **SetupSwap** and **!SetupSwapLoader** into `Interface/AddOns`.  Download the attached **SetupSwap.zip**.

See the [main repository README](https://github.com/SuttonX/SetupSwap#readme) for setup instructions, features, and compatibility details.
